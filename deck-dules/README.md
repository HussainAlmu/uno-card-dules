# Deck Duel

## Deck Duel User Story

You start with two cards and the dealer shows one. Choose **Hit** to draw a card or **Stand** to let the dealer draw.

Go over 21 and you lose. If you stand and the dealer goes over 21, you win. Otherwise, the higher total wins; a tie is a draw. Your bet changes your chips, and the game ends when you run out.

Consecutive wins increase your winning streak, which resets when you lose. The game also shows a chip goal and how many goals you have reached.

## Uno User Story

Each player starts with a hand of cards. Take turns matching the top card by color, number, or symbol; if you cannot play, draw a card. Action cards include **Skip**, **Reverse**, **Draw Two**, **Wild**, and **Wild Draw Four**. Say “UNO” when you have one card left. The first player to play all their cards wins.
